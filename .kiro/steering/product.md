# RePXL — Product Steering Document

## 1. What is RePXL?
RePXL is an e-commerce marketplace dedicated to **buying and selling vintage digital
cameras (digicams) and accessories**. It is not a general electronics store, not a
film-camera shop, not a rental platform, and not a peer-to-peer trading app. It is a
curated, admin-managed storefront that prioritizes trust, transparency, and organized
listings for a niche collector community.

## 2. Problem Statement
Existing resale channels for vintage digital cameras (social media groups, general
classifieds, broad marketplaces) suffer from:

- **No seller verification** — buyers can't confirm who they're dealing with.
- **Poor condition transparency** — listings lack standardized grading, photos are
  inconsistent, and serial numbers are rarely disclosed.
- **Disorganized discovery** — cameras are mixed with unrelated items, there's no
  filtering by brand/era/condition, and no way to compare specs side-by-side.
- **No buyer protection** — transactions happen off-platform with no recourse.

RePXL solves these by providing a structured, credible marketplace with condition
grading, serial number tracking, organized categories, and secure checkout.

## 3. Target Users

### Customers (Buyers)
- Photography and film enthusiasts seeking vintage digicams for creative use.
- Collectors looking for specific models, conditions, or eras.
- Content creators drawn to the "digicam aesthetic" for social media.
- Age range: primarily 18–35, digitally native, visually driven.
- They value: authenticity, condition transparency, curated selection, and a
  shopping experience that "gets" the culture.

### Store Admin
- Manages inventory, listings, orders, and customer communications.
- Needs: efficient dashboards, stock alerts, order tracking, listing CRUD,
  condition/serial number management.
- Values: speed, clarity, minimal friction — no decorative distractions.

## 4. Core Objectives
1. **Build buyer trust** through transparent condition grading, serial number
   disclosure, and a professional storefront that signals credibility.
2. **Organize discovery** with robust filtering (brand, era, condition, specs),
   search, and a camera comparison tool.
3. **Streamline transactions** with a secure checkout flow supporting local
   digital payment and credit card processing.
4. **Enable efficient admin operations** with a functional dashboard for
   inventory, orders, and listing management.
5. **Create an emotive brand experience** on the storefront that resonates with
   the vintage digicam community — not a sterile generic store.

## 5. Scope Boundaries

### In scope
- Customer storefront (landing, listing, PDP, compare, cart, checkout, account)
- Admin dashboard (inventory, orders, listings, analytics)
- Condition grading system (Mint / Excellent / Good / Fair)
- Serial number and authenticity tracking
- Product comparison feature
- Wishlist functionality
- Customer reviews and ratings
- Secure payment integration (local digital payment + credit card)
- Responsive design (mobile-first)

### Out of scope
- Peer-to-peer selling (sellers are admin-managed, not open marketplace)
- Film cameras or modern cameras
- Rental or subscription models
- Mobile native apps (web-only for this phase)
- International shipping logistics (single-region focus)
- AI-powered recommendations (future consideration)

## 6. Success Metrics
- Buyer conversion rate from browse to purchase
- Average time to find and compare products
- Cart abandonment rate
- Customer review submission rate
- Admin time-to-list a new product
- Zero-result search rate (indicates catalog or filter gaps)

## 7. Core Differentiators
These are the features that set RePXL apart from general marketplaces and must be
given first-class treatment in both UI and backend:

1. **Condition grading** — standardized, color-coded, prominently displayed
2. **Serial number transparency** — builds authenticity trust
3. **Camera comparison** — side-by-side spec comparison for informed decisions
4. **Curated, organized catalog** — filtered by brand, era, condition, specs
5. **Community credibility** — reviews, verified transactions, professional presentation
